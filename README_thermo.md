# Turbine Second Law

A MATLAB model of an adiabatic air turbine, built to figure out why the
Second Law still matters when the First Law balances.

## The question

[In Thermo 1 I couldn't build an intuition for the Second Law. Write 2-3
sentences in your own voice.] When I modeled this turbine, the First Law
residual was zero at every efficiency, so all the energy was accounted for.
But entropy generation said something was still "lost." If the energy is all
still in the hot exhaust, what does lost mean?
